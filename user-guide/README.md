## What can you do with Mapplex? 

Mapplex isn't just a map viewer; it is a full-stack spatial operating system. Here are five practical business models that leverage the entire ecosystem:

### 1. 🌪️ Disaster Response & Recovery (Mission Critical)
When the grid goes down, field teams cannot rely on cloud databases. 
*   **The Workflow:** Before leaving base, supervisors use the **Map View** to download local offline vector tiles of the impact zone. In the field, workers use the **Form Builder** to log destroyed buildings. Because cell towers are down, they use **Local P2P Sync (Data View)** to instantly share geometries with nearby colleagues via device-to-device meshing. Back at base, the commander uses **Geova AI Chat** to instantly draw concentric blast radii and calculate total affected acreage.

### 2. 🏙️ Smart City Asset Management (Enterprise Workforces)
Municipalities lose millions due to poor contractor tracking and fragmented data.
*   **The Workflow:** The city manager uses the **Team View** to invite temporary subcontractors as *Collectors*, ensuring they cannot delete past data. Using **Smart Logic (Forms)**, an inspection form is designed so that *if* a streetlight is marked "Broken", a cost-calculation formula is dynamically triggered. At the end of the week, the manager checks the **Performance Dashboard (Team View)** to verify the contractors met their exact geo-collection quota.

### 3. 🌱 Environmental Audits & Compliance (Intelligent Automation)
Environmental agencies waste thousands of hours manually typing out political districts and zoning codes for every wildlife sighting.
*   **The Workflow:** The agency imports their legacy district borders into the **Data View**. Using **Spatial Reference Auto-calculation (Forms)**, every time a new diseased tree is plotted, Mapplex instantly detects which district polygon it fell into and writes the ID to the form invisibly. Once the audit is done, they simply ask the **Geova AI** to: *"Analyze the logs and export a PDF report,"* receiving instant anomaly-detection warnings and a formatted stakeholder document.

### 4. 🏗️ Urban & Regional Planning (Multi-Criteria Optimization)
City planners need to determine the absolute perfect location for a new municipal facility without wading through endless desktop GIS menus.
*   **The Workflow:** The city planner imports demographic shapefiles and wetland boundaries via the **Data View**. They use **Data-Driven Symbology (Layers View)** to color-code neighborhoods by population density. To find the optimal build site for a new library, they use the **Geova AI Chat** to execute a Site Suitability DAG: *"Identify optimal construction sites that are > 5km from wetlands, < 1km from major highways, and inside high-density neighborhoods."* The AI clusters the results and generates Voronoi catchment areas for the proposed sites.

### 5. 🏥 Public Health & Population Demographics (Temporal Analytics)
Epidemiologists must track rapidly shifting disease outbreaks and alert field teams immediately to shifting resource demands.
*   **The Workflow:** Using the **Dashboard SQL Engine**, researchers run complex tabular filters to isolate specific case symptoms. To track the outbreak's movement over the last 6 months, they activate the **Time Slider (Map View)** to visualize the spread geographically. Finally, they ask the **Geova AI** to run Temporal Anomaly Detection: *"Analyze the outbreak dataset,"* and the AI proactively warns them of a 300% infection spike in a specific rural zone, allowing them to dispatch medical teams via the **Team View**.
