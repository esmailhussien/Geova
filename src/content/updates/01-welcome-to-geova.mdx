# Product updates now focus on evidence

Geova is building a connected spatial ecosystem: **Mapplex** starts in the field, **Geova AI** adds analysis and review, and project-specific systems turn spatial context into operational decisions.

This update log is the public record for that work. Each note should answer three practical questions:

- What changed?
- Why does it matter to a field, GIS, or project team?
- Is the capability available, under controlled deployment, or still being reviewed?

## What we will publish here

Updates may cover:

- Mapplex collection, synchronization, forms, media, and GIS interoperability.
- Geova AI query behavior, context awareness, review controls, and performance.
- Security, privacy, account management, and store-readiness changes.
- Documentation and operational guidance for deployed teams.
- Important limitations or rollout boundaries that users should understand.

> Product communication should be useful to the people operating the system. We will avoid invented benchmarks, unavailable packages, and claims that cannot be connected to a working release or reviewed plan.

## Current product position

Mapplex is available through controlled company deployments while public Play Store readiness work continues. Geova AI and supporting conversion services are integrated according to project configuration, permissions, and deployment requirements.
